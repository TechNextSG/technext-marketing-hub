Conversation map

How every chat on /nexi#full runs, read from the live code.

Greeting
Starter chipsThree questions plus three mini tours (3D):
Visitor asksTaps a chip or types anything. Typos, plurals and sound-alikes still match.
MatcherScores every tour and topic by keywords, then searches the site's page index.
AnswerCute opener + answer + buttons, a reaction, a camera shot and a related mini tour.
GoalThree follow-up chips; the last one is always a goal: book a call, get a quote or talk.
  • tour ≥ 1.1A mini tour wins when its score is at least 1.1 and at least as high as the best topic.
  • topic ≥ 1.1Otherwise the best topic answers when it scores 1.1 or more.
  • site pageOtherwise Nexi links up to 3 site pages that match (“Let me look that up!”).
  • weak ≥ 0.7A weak topic or tour match (0.7+) still answers.
  • fallback“Hmm, I don't have an answer for that one yet” + WhatsApp and email. Every fallback is a learning to add.

How Nexi talks

✓Cute opener, plain answer. One playful line (“Boxes! I love boxes.”), then facts in the site's wording.
✓Short. Answers under about 60 words; bold the product or step.
✓Always a next step. Every answer ends with a button or chip toward book, quote or talk.
✓Honest when unsure. Hand off to the team rather than guess.
✕No “Certified” Odoo partner. TechNext is an Odoo Ready Partner.
✕No prices, dates or promises that are not on technext.asia.
✕No ISO 27001 claim, client names or numbers beyond the approved facts.
✕No dark, heavy scenes. Light studio backgrounds; brand blue as an accent.
