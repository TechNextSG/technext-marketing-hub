Before you launch

  1. The landing page answers the ad. Every campaign points at a live solution page on technext.asia. The old /lp/ ads page now redirects to the home page, so do not use it.
  2. Use the tracking link, not the bare URL. Each placement has its own utm_source, utm_medium=paid_social (YouTube: paid_video), utm_campaign and utm_content, so GA4 shows which ad and size brought the lead.
  3. Pixels and tags. Confirm with the web team that the Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag and TikTok Pixel are on technext.asia before spending; without them the platforms cannot optimise for leads.
  4. Claims. Only the figures in the brand guide (10+ countries, 11+ enterprise clients, 3 offices, 4 AI disciplines). No prices, no “certified”, no ISO claim. The Odoo badge and the Odoo app icons go on Odoo campaigns only, unaltered: they are Odoo’s artwork, the same files technext.asia uses. App names follow odoo.com (Invoicing and Accounting are two apps).
  5. 9:16 safe zones. Turn “Platform UI on top” on and check nothing important hides under the profile row, the caption or the right-hand buttons.
  6. Video. The studio records an 8-second silent clip. Add music or a voice-over in TikTok, Meta or CapCut, and burn in captions; most people watch muted.
  7. Name ads the same way everywhere: TN_platform_campaign_placement_yyyymm, e.g. TN_linkedin_one-system_single-1x1_202610. The downloads already use this pattern.
