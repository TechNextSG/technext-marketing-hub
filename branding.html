Rules

Ten lines that catch nearly every brand mistake we have actually made.

✓
Clear space of 25% of the logo height on every side. If a capital T from the wordmark fits in the gap, you have enough.
✓
Minimum sizes: horizontal 160 px / 30 mm, vertical 90 px / 20 mm, brandmark 24 px / 6 mm. Below that, use the brandmark.
✓
Blue logo on light, white logo on dark. There is no third option.
✓
Co-branding: match visual weight not pixel size, keep each logo’s own colours, separate with clear space or a thin rule. The client leads in their proposal.
✓
Background decoration stays subtle. Fine grids and faint graphs are fine; nothing that competes with the mark.
×
Never stretch, rotate, recolour or add effects. No shadows, outlines, glows or extra gradients. Hold Shift when scaling.
×
Never retype the wordmark. The typeface is not documented; anything retyped is a near miss.
×
No low-opacity logo watermarks. Not on cards, slides or drips. Place the logo once, at full strength.
×
Never use the old all-caps TECHNEXT mark with the navy plane. Replace it wherever it turns up in an old template.
×
Never recolour the Odoo badge and never put it on website or marketing-service posts; it belongs to Odoo work only.
